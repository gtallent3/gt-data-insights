We Automate the Work That's Slowing You Down

GT Data & Insights builds workflow automations for small service businesses — contractors, landscapers, and cleaning companies. We connect the tools you already use and set up systems that handle lead follow-up, appointment reminders, and invoice chasing automatically, so you stop losing jobs and time to manual work.

What We Do

Lead Follow-Up Systems — We build automated sequences that respond to new inquiries immediately and follow up over the next few days without you touching it. You stop losing jobs to whoever responds fastest.

Scheduling & Reminder Automations — Confirmation texts, day-before reminders, and no-show follow-ups go out automatically. No more manual coordination before every job.

Invoice & Payment Follow-Up — Overdue invoices trigger automatic reminders so you stop chasing payments manually after the work is done.

Why Us

Most service business owners know they're losing time to repetitive admin work but don't know these tools exist or how to set them up. We do. We handle the full build, connect it to what you already use, and hand it off so it runs without you.
