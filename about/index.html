About GT Data & Insights

We help small business owners stop guessing and start understanding their numbers. If you run a dry cleaner, salon, laundromat, or any service-based business, you already have valuable data. You just need a better way to see it.

GT Data & Insights builds custom dashboards that turn your everyday business data into clear visuals and actionable insights. We handle the technical side so you can focus on what you do best: running your business.

What We Do

Custom Dashboards — We design dashboards around your specific business. Track revenue, customer trends, top services, peak hours, and more, all in one place.

Data That Makes Sense — No jargon, no complicated reports. Just clear charts and numbers that tell you what's working and what needs attention.

Ongoing Support — We don't just build it and walk away. Your dashboard stays updated and we're here when you need changes or have questions.

Why Us

Big companies have entire teams dedicated to understanding their data. Small businesses deserve that same advantage. We bring enterprise-level insights at a price that makes sense for a local business, because that's who we built this for.
